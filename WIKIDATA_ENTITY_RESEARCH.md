# Wikidata / Wikipedia Entity Research — Saudi Cabs GMC
*Day 23 of PLAN_30_DAY.md*

## Finding: No existing entity, and none should be created yet

Searched for a Wikidata item or Wikipedia article for "Saudi Cabs GMC" — none exists. This is expected, not a gap to rush and fill.

Wikipedia and Wikidata both require **notability**: independent, reliable, published sources writing about the business (news coverage, industry press, etc.) — not just the business's own website or social media. A small regional private taxi company doesn't meet that bar yet, and neither platform allows businesses to write their own listing to gain SEO value.

**Do not create a Wikipedia article or Wikidata item for Saudi Cabs GMC right now.** A self-created page without genuine independent coverage typically gets flagged for deletion (Wikipedia's conflict-of-interest and notability rules exist specifically for this), and a rejected/deleted page can be worse for the brand than having none. This is the same "don't fabricate credibility signals" standard already applied elsewhere on the site (no fake reviews, no fake certifications).

## What would actually make this achievable later

Notability comes from being written about by others, not from us. Realistic paths, roughly in order of plausibility for a business like this:

1. **Local/regional news coverage** — a feature or mention in a Saudi or Pakistani business/travel publication.
2. **Industry recognition** — being cited by a travel guide, tourism board content, or an established Hajj/Umrah resource as a transport option.
3. **A real, verified Google Business Profile with a track record** (Day 22) — doesn't create notability by itself, but is table stakes before anyone else would cite the business.

None of this is something I can manufacture — it has to come from real coverage happening.
